### 2. DECOY EFFECT — THREE-TIER VISIBILITY

**Status: ⚠️ PARTIAL (Family plan not live)**

The pricing page should show all three tiers side-by-side:

| Free | Pro ⭐ Most Popular | Family |
|------|---------------------|--------|
| $0 | $8/mo or $62/yr | $16/mo or $130/yr |
| 3 plans/month | Unlimited plans | Everything in Pro |
| Basic features | Full features | Up to 4 profiles |

**Psychology at work:** Family at $16 makes Pro at $8 look like a bargain. Free at $0 creates contrast that makes $8 feel minimal.
