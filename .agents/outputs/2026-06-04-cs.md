### Fix Recommendation for Quinn

**Priority: Profile completion audit — required before any marketing spend or public launch.**

Specifically:

Go into the signup flow right now as a new user and answer these questions:

1. Can a user skip the profile step and generate a plan immediately? If yes — this is a problem. The first plan will be generic. A generic first plan is a weak first impression and will hurt D1 retention.
2. If profile completion is required, is the form short enough that a user will finish it? More than 4-5 fields before the first plan is too much friction. Recommend: name, dietary preference (dropdown), one health goal (dropdown), calorie target (optional). That is it.
3. Is there a progress indicator? Users abandon multi-step forms when they cannot see the end. A simple "Step 1 of 2" label costs nothing and reduces drop-off.
4. After the first plan is generated, is there a clear save action? If saving requires another step the user does not expect, plan saves will be low — and saved plans are a strong predictor of return visits.

### Email Improvement for Jordan

**Priority: Build the 3-email onboarding sequence. This is the most important pre-launch task on the email side.**

No sequence exists. When new users arrive — whether from organic search, Product Hunt, or any other source — there is no follow-up. A user who signs up, generates one plan, and never hears from Mealzy again has no reason to return.

Here is the sequence to build:

**Email 1 — Sent immediately after signup**
Subject: Your first meal plan is ready
Content: Welcome, confirm what Mealzy does in two sentences, one clear CTA to generate their first plan if they have not already. Warm and direct. No features list, no upsell.

**Email 2 — Sent 24 hours after signup (D1)**
Subject: How did your first plan go?
Content: Short. Ask if the plan matched their preferences. Link back to the app. If they have not yet generated a plan, this is a soft nudge. If they have, this is a retention touch. No upsell yet.

**Email 3 — Sent 5 days after signup**
Subject: You have 2 plans left this month
Content: Soft reminder of the free tier limit. Not a scare tactic — frame it as "make the most of your month." Introduce Pro naturally: one sentence, one link to the upgrade page. This is the first appropriate moment to mention paid.

Platform note: Jordan should confirm what email tool is being used. If none is set up yet, that is the prerequisite. Mailchimp free tier or Resend are both reasonable choices for this stage.
