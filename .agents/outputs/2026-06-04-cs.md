### Fix Recommendation for Quinn

**Observation:** When new users do arrive, the profile setup step is the highest-risk drop-off point before they see any value from the product. There is currently no data confirming that users complete it.

**Recommendation for Quinn:** Add a visible progress indicator during the profile/onboarding step — something minimal, like "Step 1 of 2: Tell us about your diet." This reduces abandonment by making the end feel close and reachable. Additionally, consider whether profile completion is required before generating a first plan, or optional. If it is required, that is a significant barrier; if skippable, users may generate a generic plan and then refine — which could actually improve D1 retention. Flagging this as worth one quick UX audit before traffic arrives.

### Email Improvement for Jordan

**Current state:** No onboarding email sequence exists or has been sent. This is actually the highest-leverage email opportunity available right now.

**Recommendation for Jordan:** Build a 3-email onboarding sequence before the next wave of signups, so it fires automatically from day one. Suggested structure:

- **Email 1 — sent immediately at signup:** Confirms the account. One sentence on what Mealzy does. Single CTA: "Build your first meal plan" linking to mealzy.org/dashboard.html. No upsell yet.
- **Email 2 — sent at D2 (if user has not yet generated a plan):** Subject line: "Your first meal plan takes 30 seconds." Body: short, warm, direct. Remind them the free tier gives them 3 plans. CTA back to the app.
- **Email 3 — sent at D5 (after first plan is generated, or as a nudge if not):** This is the first appropriate moment to introduce Pro. Frame it around what they cannot do yet — PDF export, unlimited plans, Mia coach. Keep it factual, not pushy. CTA: "See what Pro includes" linking to mealzy.org/upgrade.html.

Voice across all three: warm, calm, direct. No emojis. No "transform" or "game-changer." Write like a friend who knows nutrition and wants to help.
