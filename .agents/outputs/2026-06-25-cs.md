**Platform recommendation:** Loops (loops.so) — built for SaaS, native Stripe integration, simple trigger logic, free tier sufficient for current volume, no overengineering required at this stage.

**Email 1 — Send immediately on signup**
- Subject: Your first meal plan is ready
- Word count: 80–100 words
- Content: One sentence of welcome. One sentence on what Mealzy does. One CTA button: "Generate my first plan" → mealzy.org/dashboard.html
- Tone: Warm, direct. No feature list. No long intro. Get them into the product.
- Suppression: Skip if user has already generated a plan before email sends.

**Email 2 — Send at 48 hours post-signup if no plan has been generated**
- Subject: Takes about 30 seconds
- Word count: 100–120 words
- Content: Acknowledge they haven't started yet. One concrete sentence on what they'll get (a week of meals matched to what they eat and how much time they have). One CTA: "See what Mealzy builds for you" → mealzy.org/dashboard.html
- Tone: No pressure. No urgency language. Just a quiet nudge.
- Suppression: Skip if plan has been generated.

**Email 3 — Send at day 5 post-signup if no upgrade has occurred**
- Subject: What Pro gets you (and what it costs)
- Word count: 120–150 words
- Content: Name the three Pro features that matter most — unlimited plans, PDF export, AI coach Mia. State the price plainly: $8/month. One CTA: "See Pro" → mealzy.org/upgrade.html
- Tone: No hype. No "don't miss out." Just a clear, honest description of what the upgrade is.
- Suppression: Skip if already Pro.

**Trigger logic summary:**
- Email 1: signup event
- Email 2: 48h after signup AND plan_count = 0
- Email 3: day 5 after signup AND subscription_tier = free
