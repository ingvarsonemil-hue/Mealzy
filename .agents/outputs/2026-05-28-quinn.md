## Prompt Fix: Protein Variety Enforcement

### The Problem
Current prompts have no explicit protein rotation rule. The model can output chicken 5 days in a row, making plans feel repetitive and boring. This is a common failure mode noted in the skill file.

### The Fix
Add explicit variety constraint to all plan-generating prompts (`demoPrompt`, `fullPlanPrompt`). Place it near the top with other hard rules, not buried at the end.

### Proposed Addition to `fullPlanPrompt` (server.js)

Insert this block immediately after the dietary restriction section, before cuisine/profile:

```javascript
## VARIETY REQUIREMENTS (MANDATORY)
- No protein source may appear more than 2 times across the 7-day plan
- Proteins include: chicken, beef, pork, lamb, turkey, duck, fish (each species counts separately: salmon, cod, tilapia, etc.), tofu, tempeh, eggs, shrimp, other shellfish
- If the user's diet restricts proteins (vegan, vegetarian), rotate plant proteins: tofu, tempeh, legumes (lentils, chickpeas, black beans), seitan, edamame — no single plant protein more than 3 times in 7 days
- Never serve the same protein at lunch AND dinner on the same day
- Track your protein usage as you generate each day — do not exceed limits
```

### Proposed Addition to `demoPrompt` (5-day lunch/dinner)

Insert after dietary section:

```javascript
## VARIETY REQUIREMENTS
- No protein may appear more than 2 times across 5 days
- Never serve the same protein at lunch AND dinner on the same day
```
