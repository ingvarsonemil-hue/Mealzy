## YOUR NORTH STAR

Every page must pass the 5-second test: a cold visitor can understand what Mealzy is and why they should care within 5 seconds. If they can't — rewrite until they can.

Conversion goal: free visitor → signup → plan generated → Pro upgrade.
Every word on every page either moves someone toward that goal or it doesn't earn its place.

---

## MEALZY BRAND RULES (non-negotiable)

Read `brand-voice.md` before writing anything.

**Voice:** Warm, calm, direct. Friend who knows nutrition — never wellness influencer, never salesy.

**Always:**
- Benefits over features (outcomes, not functions)
- Specific numbers: "30 seconds," "$8/month," "7 meals," "unlimited plans"
- Active voice, real verbs
- Clarity over cleverness — if it sounds clever, rewrite it plain

**Never:**
- "Level up," "game-changer," "transform your life"
- "Clean eating," "guilt-free," "superfoods"
- Fake urgency: "Limited time!" "Don't miss out!"
- Emojis
- Passive voice
- Jargon

---

## THE 5 COPYWRITING PRINCIPLES

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — if it sounds clever, make it plain
2. **Benefits over features** — "plan your whole week in 30 seconds" not "AI meal generation"
3. **Specificity over vagueness** — "saves 2 hours every Sunday" not "saves time"
4. **Customer language** — use words users say, not words Mealzy uses
5. **One idea per section** — never two things fighting for attention

## CRO ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

Analyze every page in this order (highest impact first):

### 1. Value Proposition Clarity
- Can a visitor understand what Mealzy is and why they should care in 5 seconds?
- Is the primary benefit clear, specific, and differentiated?
- Is it in the customer's language?

### 2. Headline
- Does it communicate the core value prop?
- Is it specific? Does it contain a number or concrete outcome?
- Does it match where the visitor came from?

**Headline formats that work:**
- Outcome-focused: "Plan your whole week in 30 seconds"
- Specificity: "Cut your grocery bill by $60/month"
- Social proof: "Join [X] people who stopped planning meals by hand"

### 3. CTA Copy
Weak → Strong:
- "Sign Up" → "Start Planning Free"
- "Submit" → "Get My Meal Plan"
- "Upgrade" → "Unlock Unlimited Plans"
- "Learn More" → "See How It Works"

One clear primary CTA. Repeated at key scroll points. Value-driven, not action-driven.

### 4. Trust Signals
Near every CTA:
- User count or plans generated
- "Free — no credit card"
- "Cancel anytime"

### 5. Objection Handling
The three objections every free user has before upgrading:
1. "Is $8/month worth it?" → Show what unlimited plans + grocery export + AI coach is worth
2. "Will this actually work for me?" → Show dietary restriction support, custom macros
3. "What if I don't like it?" → "Cancel anytime. No questions."

### 6. Friction
- Form fields: fewer is always better
- Button states: loading indicator when clicked
- Mobile: does it work at 375px?

## PAGE-SPECIFIC RULES

### Homepage (index.html)
- Headline: outcome-focused, specific number, 8 words max
- Subheadline: explains HOW in one sentence
- CTA above fold: "Start Planning Free — No Credit Card"
- Section order: Problem → Solution → How it works → Social proof → CTA

### Pricing Page (pricing.html)
- Show annual plan first (anchor high)
- Highlight recommended plan with visual indicator
- Answer "which plan is right for me?" before they ask
- CTA on each plan: specific ("Start 7-Day Free Trial"), not generic ("Choose Plan")

### Upgrade Page (upgrade.html)
- Headline: "You've used your 3 free plans"
- Subhead: what they're missing, specifically
- Primary CTA: "Start 7-Day Free Trial — $8/month"
- Secondary: "See annual plan (save 35%)"
- Dismiss: "Remind me later" — never hide it
- Social proof: near the CTA, not at the bottom

### 402 Upgrade Modal
Copy template:
```
Headline: "You've used your 3 free plans this month"
Subtext: "Upgrade to Pro — unlimited plans, grocery lists, PDF export, AI coach"
Primary CTA: "Start 7-Day Free Trial — $8/month"
Secondary: "See annual plan (save 35%)"
Dismiss: "Remind me later"
```
No dark patterns. Easy to close. Trust = future conversion.

### Exit-Intent Popup
Two-stage:
- Stage 1: $8/month offer
- Stage 2 (if dismissed): $4/month discounted offer
Copy must acknowledge the dismissal: "Still not sure? Try it for $4/month — cancel anytime."
