## MEALZY BRAND RULES (non-negotiable)

Read `brand-voice.md` before writing anything.

**Email-specific voice:** "Note from a friend." Not a newsletter. Not a marketing blast.
**Subject lines:** Short and specific. Under 60 characters. Benefit-driven. Never clickbait.
**Body:** 150–500 words. One purpose per email. Value before ask. Always.

**Never:**
- Guilt language: "You haven't logged in today!" / "Don't let your streak die!"
- Fake urgency: "Limited time!" / "Last chance!"
- Emojis anywhere
- More than one CTA per email
- Passive voice

## THE 5 EMAIL SEQUENCES

### SEQUENCE 1 — Welcome (triggers: on signup)

**Email 1 — Immediate (0 min after signup)**
- Subject: "Your Mealzy account is ready"
- Purpose: get them to generate their first plan RIGHT NOW
- Body: 3 sentences max. What Mealzy does. One big button: "Generate My First Plan"
- Tone: calm, warm, direct

**Email 2 — Day 1 (24hrs after signup, if no plan generated)**
- Subject: "Takes 30 seconds — here's how Mealzy works"
- Purpose: remove any confusion about what to do first
- Body: 3-step walkthrough (Enter your goals → Get your plan → Download grocery list). CTA: "Try it now — free"

**Email 3 — Day 3 (if still on free plan, 2 plans used)**
- Subject: "You've got 1 free plan left this month"
- Purpose: soft nudge toward Pro before they hit the wall
- Body: what Pro unlocks (unlimited plans, grocery export, AI coach, PDF). Price anchor: $8/month = less than one coffee. CTA: "See Pro plans"

### SEQUENCE 2 — Limit Hit (triggers: 3rd plan generated → 402 returned)

**Email 1 — Immediate**
- Subject: "You hit your free limit — here's 7 days of Pro free"
- Purpose: convert at peak motivation (they just wanted another plan)
- Body: acknowledge they hit the limit → what Pro gives them → trial offer. CTA: "Start Free Trial"
- This is the highest-leverage email. Send within 5 minutes of the 402 event.

**Email 2 — 48hrs later (if no upgrade)**
- Subject: "Still thinking about it?"
- Purpose: address the hesitation
- Body: answer the 3 objections — worth it? works for me? what if I don't like it? CTA: "Try Pro free for 7 days"

### SEQUENCE 3 — Day 3 Nudge (triggers: 3 days after signup, not upgraded)

**Email 1**
- Subject: "3 days in — here's what Pro users do differently"
- Purpose: social proof + feature highlight
- Body: 2-3 specific things Pro users do that free users can't (unlimited plans, AI coach, grocery export). Real use case. CTA: "Unlock Pro — $8/month"

### SEQUENCE 4 — Weekly Digest (triggers: every Sunday, all active free users)

**Template:**
- Subject: "Your Mealzy meal plan for this week"
- Purpose: keep Mealzy top of mind + soft Pro upsell
- Body:
  - Teaser of a sample plan for the week (2-3 meals, appetizing)
  - One-click "Generate My Plan" button
  - Bottom: soft upsell — "Want unlimited plans + grocery lists? Pro is $8/month"
- Send: Sunday 9am in user's timezone

### SEQUENCE 5 — Streak Protection (triggers: 6 days since last plan generated)

**Email 1**
- Subject: "You haven't planned this week yet"
- Purpose: re-engage before they churn
- Body: 2 sentences. Remind them what they were working toward (their goal). CTA: "Plan this week — takes 30 seconds"
- Tone: friendly reminder, not guilt. Never say "Your streak is about to break!"

### SEQUENCE 6 — Re-engagement (triggers: 14+ days inactive, Pro user)

**Email 1**
- Subject: "Still there? We added something for you"
- Purpose: remind them what they're paying for
- Body: 1 new or underused feature highlight. CTA: "Try it now"

**Email 2 — 7 days later (still inactive)**
- Subject: "Before you cancel — one thing"
- Purpose: last chance to show value before churn
- Body: genuine. Acknowledge they haven't been back. Ask what got in the way (survey link). Offer to pause instead of cancel.

## SUBJECT LINE RULES

- Under 60 characters
- Specific number or outcome when possible
- No clickbait, no fake urgency
- No emojis
- Test: would a friend send this subject line in a real email?

**Strong:** "You've got 1 free plan left this month" / "Takes 30 seconds — here's how it works"
**Weak:** "Don't miss out!!" / "Your meal planning game just changed" / "We have exciting news"

## SOCIAL POST RULES

Every week, write one Facebook post for the Mealzy page.

**Format:** Include a `## SOCIAL POST` section at the end of your output.
**Length:** 3–5 sentences max.
**Tone:** Helpful and direct — same as emails, but even shorter.
**Content:** Rotate weekly between:
- Meal planning tip (e.g. "How to plan a week of meals in under 5 minutes")
- Feature highlight (e.g. "Did you know Mealzy tracks macros automatically?")
- User benefit story (e.g. "One Mealzy user cut her grocery bill by 30%...")
- Question/engagement (e.g. "What's the hardest part of eating healthy for you?")

**Always end with:** mealzy.org

**Never:** emojis, hashtags, clickbait, fake urgency.

**Example:**
```
Most people skip meal planning because it takes too long. Mealzy does it in 30 seconds — enter your calorie goal, hit generate, and your whole week is planned. Free to try. mealzy.org
```

## REFLEXION PROTOCOL

Read `.agents/reflexion.md` for the full protocol. This section defines Jordan's specific success criteria and failure patterns.

**Memory file**: `.agents/memory/jordan.md` — read this at the start of every session.

### Success Criteria (Evaluator)
Score each email written 1–10 against:
- Open rate: subject line should achieve ≥25% open rate (3 pts)
- Click rate: CTA should achieve ≥3% CTR (3 pts)
- Unsubscribe rate: must stay below 0.5% (2 pts)
- Tone: warm, personal, reads like a human — not a marketing blast (2 pts)

For new emails with no data yet, score on predicted performance based on past reflections.

### Common Failure Patterns (Reflector seeds)
- "Free" or "urgent" in subject line → spam filter, open rate collapses
- Multiple CTAs in one email → user paralysis, clicks nothing
- Email too long — users read the first 3 lines only
- Sent on a Monday morning — inboxes are full, gets buried
- Generic greeting ("Hi there") instead of using the user's name

### Reflection Trigger
After every email campaign sent (with real open/click data). Write one reflection per campaign using actual performance numbers, not estimates. If open rate < 20%, mandatory reflection before next send.
