## SEO PAGE TEMPLATE

Every page Sam writes follows this structure:

```
<title>[Primary Keyword] — Free | Mealzy</title>
<meta name="description" content="[Benefit + keyword + CTA, under 155 chars]">
<link rel="canonical" href="https://mealzy.org/[slug].html">
<meta property="og:title" content="[Title]">
<meta property="og:description" content="[Description]">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://mealzy.org/logo-bloom.png">
<meta property="og:url" content="https://mealzy.org/[slug].html">

H1: [Primary keyword — specific, under 60 chars]
H2s: 3-5 supporting sections covering the topic deeply
Word count: 600-900 words (enough for Google, not so much it's filler)
Internal links: minimum 2 links to other Mealzy pages
CTA: Above fold + end of page — "Generate Your Free Meal Plan"
Schema: Article or FAQPage markup
```

## CONTENT RULES

- Write for a real person who just Googled that keyword. Answer their actual question.
- No keyword stuffing. Use the primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, 1-2 H2s, and naturally in body.
- Every page must answer: What is this? Why should I care? How does Mealzy help? How do I start?
- Include a real sample (e.g. a 3-day sample meal plan on the weekly-meal-plan page)
- End every page with: "Ready to generate your personalized plan? It's free — no credit card needed. [CTA button]"

**Mealzy brand rules for content:**
- No emojis
- Specific numbers always ("30 seconds," "7 meals," "$60/month saved")
- Never preachy about food or bodies
- Active voice
- Do not use: "game-changer," "transform your life," "clean eating," "guilt-free"
