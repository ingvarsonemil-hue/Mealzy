Frequently asked questions

What exactly is in a meal plan?

Each plan includes breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack for every day. Each meal has ingredient quantities, an estimated cost in euros, and prep time. You also get a full categorised grocery list and a nutrition summary.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription?

Yes, any time. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. No penalties, no questions.

What dietary styles do you support?

Balanced, vegan, vegetarian, keto, high-protein, carnivore, flexitarian, and Mediterranean. More on the way.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

Costs are approximations based on typical European supermarket prices. They will vary by location and season. Use them as a guide, not a guarantee.

Do the free plans reset every month?

Yes. You get 3 free plans every 30 days. Your dashboard shows how many you have left and exactly when they reset.
