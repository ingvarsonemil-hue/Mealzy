Common questions

Can I cancel at any time? +

Yes — cancel in one click from your Stripe billing portal. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. No penalties, no fine print.

What happens to my free plan when it runs out? +

Your 3 free generations reset automatically every 30 days from the date of your first generation in that cycle. You never lose access to plans you have already generated.

Is my data safe? +

Yes. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. All traffic is served over HTTPS. We do not sell your data to third parties. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your card number.

Do you store my meal plans? +

Your meal plans and profile preferences are stored securely on our servers, tied to your account. You can access them from any device as long as you're signed in. We do not sell your data.
