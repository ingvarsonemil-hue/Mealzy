How the $50 budget ceiling works

When you set a $50 weekly limit, Mealzy does not estimate costs after selecting meals — it enforces the cap during selection. Each candidate meal is evaluated for its cost contribution to the running grocery total. If a meal would push the total above $50, it is replaced before being added to the plan. The process continues until all 7 days are filled and the cumulative grocery cost is within the $50 ceiling.

Mealzy's algorithm naturally selects meals built around high-yield staples: dried legumes, whole grains, eggs, and seasonal produce. The plan will lean on these categories to stay within your ceiling without sacrificing nutritional completeness.

For even tighter budgets, try combining the $50 cap with Mealzy's zero-waste mode: zero-waste meal planning ensures every purchased ingredient is consumed, so nothing is wasted and nothing is over-bought.

Frequently asked questions

What if grocery prices in my area are higher?

Mealzy localizes cost estimates to your region. If your local prices make $50 infeasible for 7 full dinners, the plan will reflect that — you will see which items push costs up and can adjust your budget ceiling accordingly.

Can I set a different budget ceiling?

Yes. The $50 limit is one example. You can set any weekly spend limit — $35, $75, $100 — and Mealzy will apply the same hard-cap logic to that amount.
