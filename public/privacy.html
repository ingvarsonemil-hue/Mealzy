Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who we are

Mealzy is an AI-powered meal planning service operated as a personal project. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at the email address on your account confirmation.

2. What data we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and (in future) to send you product updates you opt into.
  • Password — stored as a one-way bcrypt hash. We cannot read your password.
  • Name — optional display name you provide at registration.
  • Usage count — how many meal plans you have generated this month, to enforce the free plan limit.
  • Subscription status — whether your account has an active Pro subscription, provided to us by Stripe after payment.
  • Meal plan preferences — budget, dietary style, and goal inputs you enter when generating a plan. These are sent to the AI model and are not stored persistently on our servers.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not run analytics scripts (no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar).
  • We do not store the meal plans generated for you — they are displayed in your browser and then discarded.

4. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you when you log in.
  • To track free plan usage and enforce the monthly limit.
  • To process your subscription payment via Stripe (we never see your card details).
  • To send transactional emails such as registration confirmation (if email is configured).

5. Third-party services

  • Stripe — handles all payment processing. Your card data goes directly to Stripe and is never stored on our servers. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • Anthropic — our AI provider. Your meal plan preferences are sent to the Anthropic API to generate your plan. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Render — our hosting provider. Your data is stored on servers managed by Render.com in the United States.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you want your account and all associated data deleted, contact us and we will action it within 7 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is httpOnly and contains no personal data — only a session identifier. We also use localStorage in your browser to remember onboarding state (e.g. whether you have seen the upgrade prompt). This data never leaves your device.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Mealzy after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
