# Contact Mealzy

Mealzy is run by one person, Emil Ingvarson, from Uppsala, Sweden. Every message
is read by the founder.

## Email

**support@mealzy.org** — questions, bug reports, feature requests, billing, and
account help all go to this address. Replies come within 24 hours on weekdays.

When reporting a bug, include: your account email, what you were doing, and what
happened instead.

## Common requests

- **Pro didn't activate.** Pro usually activates within 60 seconds. If it hasn't
  after 5 minutes, email your payment confirmation and it will be activated
  manually within a few hours.
- **Refund.** A full refund is available within 7 days of subscribing. Email the
  request with your payment confirmation.
- **Delete my account and data.** Email support@mealzy.org; data is deleted
  within 30 days of the request.
- **Password reset.** Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page at
  https://mealzy.org/login.html.
