# About Mealzy

Mealzy is a budget-first AI meal planner. It exists to solve one problem: meal
planners that ignore your grocery budget.

## Why it was built

Most meal planners treat your grocery budget as an afterthought — they suggest
recipes first and let the cost land wherever it lands. Mealzy works the other way
around: you set a weekly budget, and every plan it builds has to stay inside it.
That is the whole idea.

> "I built Mealzy because meal planning was eating my Sundays. No spreadsheet.
> No guessing. Just a plan that works." — Emil Ingvarson

## Who runs it

Mealzy is built by Emil Ingvarson, a solo founder, 16 years old, based in
Uppsala, Sweden. He writes the code, generates the meal data, and runs the
servers himself. It is a small operation: changes ship fast, and feedback goes
straight to the person who can act on it.

## Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests all go to the same inbox, read by
the founder: **support@mealzy.org**. Replies come within 24 hours on weekdays.

## Legitimacy and limitations

- Payments are processed by Stripe. Mealzy never sees or stores card numbers.
- Meal generation uses Anthropic's Claude API. Your name and email are never sent
  to Anthropic.
- Mealzy is an AI tool and does not provide medical or nutritional advice.
- Grocery price figures are estimates, not quotes.
