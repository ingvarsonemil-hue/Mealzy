# Mealzy — Meal Plans That Stay Inside Your Grocery Budget

Mealzy is a free web app that plans a full week of meals to fit inside a grocery
budget you set, then writes the grocery list for you. A personalized week is
generated in about 30 seconds.

## What it does

- **Budget-first planning.** You set a weekly grocery spend limit. Every
  ingredient is priced as the plan is assembled, so the seven days you get back
  already add up to less than your cap — the budget is a constraint the plan is
  built against, not a total handed to you afterwards.
- **Calorie and macro targets.** Calorie targets use the Mifflin–St Jeor
  energy-needs formula based on the height, weight, and activity level you enter.
  Plans can also hit protein, carb, and fat targets.
- **Diet handling.** Balanced, high-protein, vegan, and keto, plus allergies and
  any ingredients you want kept out. Your choice applies to every meal in the
  week, not a filter over a fixed plan.
- **One grocery list.** All meals consolidate into a single, deduplicated list,
  sorted by category, with a price estimate per item. Tick items off as you shop.
- **Swap any meal** in one click; the plan and list stay inside budget.

## How it works

1. Tell Mealzy your calories, weekly budget, diet, and anything you can't eat —
   a few quick steps, once.
2. Get your week in about 30 seconds.
3. Shop from the list, cook, repeat.

## Pricing

- **Free** — one new meal plan a day for your first 30 days, then one new plan
  each week after that, for as long as you use Mealzy. No credit card.
- **Pro** — $8/month or $62/year. Removes the plan limit.
- **Family** — $16/month or $128/year. Adds up to 4 profiles.

## Important notes

- Grocery prices are estimates from typical supermarket rates, shown as ranges.
  They are not quotes; your local prices may vary.
- Nutrition values are estimates and are not medical or nutritional advice.
- There is no public API for meal generation. Use the web app at
  https://mealzy.org.
