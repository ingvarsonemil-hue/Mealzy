What is an AI meal plan generator?

An AI meal plan generator uses artificial intelligence to create a personalised weekly meal plan based on your specific goals, dietary preferences, and budget. Unlike generic meal plans you find in magazines, an AI generator adapts to your exact calorie target, macros, allergies, and cooking skill level.

Mealzy is an AI meal plan generator that takes about 30 seconds to produce a full 7-day plan with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack for each day — plus a complete grocery list with estimated costs.

How does the Mealzy meal plan generator work?

Mealzy uses Claude AI (by Anthropic) to generate meal plans. Here's the process:

  • You enter your daily calorie goal, weekly grocery budget, cooking skill, and any dietary restrictions or allergies.
  • Mealzy sends this to Claude with a detailed prompt that includes macro targets, budget constraints, and dietary requirements.
  • Claude generates a 7-day meal plan that respects all your constraints — typically in under 30 seconds.
  • Mealzy formats the output into a readable plan with a sorted grocery list, calorie counts, and per-meal costs.

The result is practical, home-cooked meals using ingredients you can find in any regular supermarket.

What diets does the meal plan generator support?

Mealzy supports all major dietary preferences and restrictions, including:

  • Vegetarian and vegan
  • Gluten-free and coeliac-safe
  • Dairy-free
  • Keto and low-carb
  • Paleo

You can also specify a preferred cuisine style — Mediterranean, Asian, Indian, Mexican, Scandinavian, and more — and Mealzy will lean into that style while respecting all your other constraints.

How accurate are the calorie counts?

Mealzy uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with your age, weight, height, and activity level to calculate your daily calorie target. Meal plans are generated to hit that target within about 50 kcal per day. Individual meal calorie estimates are approximate — like any home cooking, the actual amount varies based on your exact ingredients and portion sizes.
