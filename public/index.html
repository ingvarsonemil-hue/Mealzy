Why I built this

Hi, I'm Emil.

I'm 16 and I live in Sweden. In 2024 I was spending every Sunday making a colour-coded spreadsheet of what to eat that week — matching macros, checking my budget, writing out the grocery list by hand. It took two hours, every single week.

I knew AI could do most of this automatically, so I built a basic version for myself one evening. It generated a plan in 30 seconds that used to take me all afternoon. I thought: other people probably hate this too.

Mealzy is that tool, made a bit more reliable. I work on it between school and football practice. There's no team, no investors, no office. If something's broken, that's on me. If it saves you time this week, that's enough.

— Emil
