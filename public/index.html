FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mealzy really free?

Yes! Our free plan includes up to 7 AI-generated meals per week, basic nutrition tracking, and access to the AI coach with 10 messages/day. Premium unlocks unlimited meals, advanced analytics, and more.

Can Mealzy handle my dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Mealzy supports vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, keto, paleo, dairy-free, nut-free, and many more dietary preferences and allergies. Just tell us in onboarding!

How accurate are the calorie calculations?

We use the scientifically validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation combined with your activity level to calculate your TDEE. Our AI then generates meals that hit your targets within ±50 calories per day.

Do I need to cook every meal myself?

No! You can set your cooking time preference (even 5 minutes!) and Mealzy will suggest meals that fit your schedule, including quick options and no-cook meals.
