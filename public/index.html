FAQ

Common questions.

Is it actually free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 3 meal plans per month, the grocery list, calorie and macro tracking, and 10 AI coach messages per day. No credit card required. Pro is $8/month for unlimited everything.

How does the budget work?

You set a weekly grocery budget in onboarding. Mealzy builds every meal plan to stay within it. The AI calculates per-meal costs and won't suggest meals that push you over.

Can it handle dietary restrictions?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, keto, paleo, dairy-free, nut-free, and more. Set your preferences and allergies in onboarding and they apply to every generated plan.

How accurate are the calorie calculations?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with your activity level to calculate your daily target. Meals are generated to hit that target within about 50 calories per day.

What languages does Mealzy support?

English, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, and Japanese. The meal names, ingredient lists, AI coach responses, and app interface all switch to your chosen language.
