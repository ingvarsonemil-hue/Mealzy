How the zero-waste algorithm works

Before finalizing any meal plan, Mealzy runs an ingredient-reconciliation pass across all 7 days. Every item on the projected grocery list is checked against every meal in the plan. Where an ingredient appears in only one meal and would be purchased in excess of what that meal needs, the algorithm substitutes or adds a second meal that consumes the remainder. This runs iteratively until no ingredient is projected to have leftover quantity at week's end.

Zero-waste planning vs. standard meal planning

Standard meal planners list recipes independently. A recipe calling for a bunch of cilantro and another calling for a bunch of parsley means two separate herb purchases, each used once. Mealzy's zero-waste mode cross-references those recipes and, where possible, substitutes one herb for another or adds a third meal that uses both — eliminating the excess purchase at the source.

Frequently asked questions

Does zero-waste mode work with dietary restrictions?

Yes. The ingredient-recycling logic operates on top of whatever dietary filters you set. If you select vegetarian, the algorithm only cross-references vegetarian-compatible meals when eliminating leftover ingredients.

Can I see which ingredients are shared across meals?

Yes. The generated grocery list groups shared ingredients and shows which meals each item appears in, so you can verify the overlap before shopping.
