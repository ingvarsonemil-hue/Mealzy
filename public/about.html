Why I built Mealzy.

One person, one problem: meal planners that ignore your grocery budget.

"I built Mealzy because meal planning was eating my Sundays. No spreadsheet. No guessing. Just a plan that works."
E
Emil Ingvarson
Founder · 16 years old · Uppsala, Sweden

Most meal planners treat your grocery budget as an afterthought — they suggest recipes first and let the cost land wherever it lands. Mealzy works the other way around: you set a weekly budget, and every plan it builds has to stay inside it. That's the whole idea.

I'm building Mealzy on my own, from Uppsala, Sweden — writing the code, generating the meal data, and running the servers myself. It's a small operation. That means changes ship fast, but it also means I'm still working out what people actually need.

If Mealzy saves you a Sunday, or keeps you under budget at the store this week, it's doing its job. If it doesn't, tell me — I read every message.
