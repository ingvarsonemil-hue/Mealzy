# Mealzy — Creator Brief
For anyone making a video about Mealzy. Read this before you shoot anything.

---

## What Mealzy is (one sentence)

Mealzy is a free AI meal planner — you tell it your goals and weekly grocery budget, it builds a full week of meals, hits your macros, and writes the grocery list in about 30 seconds.

---

## The five things that must be in every video

These are not suggestions. Every video that skips one of these has scored below 60/100. Every video that hits all five has a real shot at 80+.

### 1. Bold on-screen text — appears at 0:00, before you speak

One line. Under 8 words. High contrast text on screen the moment the video starts.
This is the hook for everyone watching with the sound off (most people on TikTok).

**Use one of these exactly:**
- `You're wasting 2 hours every Sunday.`
- `I cut my grocery bill by $60.`
- `30 seconds. Whole week planned.`
- `Stop planning meals by hand.`
- `This took me 28 seconds.`
- `Meal planning binders are dead.`

Do not invent your own until you've tested one of these first.

### 2. A disruptive opening action — happens in the first 0.5 seconds

Do not open by standing still or sitting in a normal setting.
Something unexpected needs to happen in the first half second.

**Actions that work:**
- Throw a stack of printed meal plans or a binder off the counter in frustration, then turn to camera
- Slam a phone face-down (showing a chaotic notes app or spreadsheet), then look at camera
- Hold up a phone showing a messy grocery spreadsheet, shake your head, then flip camera toward yourself
- Pull out earbuds mid-frustration as if you've been stressing about it, turn to camera

The action earns the stop. The text confirms why they should stay.

### 3. Your first spoken sentence — problem AND payoff together

Do not open with a greeting. Do not open with your name. Do not open with "So I found this app."

Your first sentence must contain both the problem and the result. Specifically — a real number in both halves.

**Say one of these:**
- "I used to spend 90 minutes on meal planning every Sunday. Now it takes 30 seconds."
- "You're wasting 2 hours every week on meal planning. This does it in 30 seconds."
- "I was spending more time logging food than actually working out. Mealzy handles it in 30 seconds."
- "I cut my grocery bill by $60 a month just by letting Mealzy plan the week."

Do not say "This was me until I found Mealzy." — that scores 17/30.
Do not say "I don't usually recommend apps. I'm recommending this one." — that scores 6/30 unless the number follows immediately.

### 4. Show the app for at least 3 seconds

At some point between 5 and 18 seconds, turn your phone toward the camera and show Mealzy generating or displaying a meal plan. You don't need to narrate every detail. Just show it working. If you have a second device, a quick screen recording overlay works too.

### 5. End with the CTA — calm, one line, no pressure

"It's free at mealzy.org." That's it. Nothing more. No "click the link in bio!!" No "hurry before it's gone." Just the line, then let it breathe for half a second before the video ends.

---

## What emotion to play

Pick one and stay in it for the first 5 seconds:

- **Mild frustration** — you know how annoying the old way was. You're not dramatic about it. Just done with it.
- **Quiet relief** — you found the fix. You're not performing excitement. Just telling someone something true.
- **Dry amusement** — you can't believe you didn't find this sooner. Slightly deadpan.

Do not perform enthusiasm. Do not smile during the problem statement. The smile lands after the solution.

---

## Video structure — full template

```
[0:00] ON-SCREEN TEXT hits before you speak
[0:00–0:02] Disruptive physical action
[0:00–0:03] Spoken hook — problem + payoff + number, first sentence
[0:03–0:15] Bridge — 2–3 claims, one per sentence. Show the app.
            Cover: 30 seconds / budget holds / grocery list included / free
[0:15–0:22] Payoff — one honest personal result
[0:22–0:25] CTA — "It's free at mealzy.org." Then silence.
```

Total length: 22–28 seconds is the sweet spot.

---

## Things not to say

- "Game-changer" / "level up" / "transform your life"
- "Clean eating" / "guilt-free" / "cheat meal"
- "You won't believe..." / "What if I told you..."
- "WARNING:" in caps / "98% of people" (we don't use fake stats)
- "Don't miss out!" / "Click the link in bio now!!!"
- Anything that implies food is morally "good" or "bad"

---

## Scoring target

Every video gets scored by Blotato before it goes live. The target is 80+/100. The rubric:

| Dimension | Points | How to hit it |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Scroll-stopper | 35 | Text at 0:00 + disruptive opening action |
| Problem/benefit | 30 | Specific number in first sentence, payoff not buried |
| Sound test | 5 | Text overlay handles it |
| Info density | 15 | 2–3 specific claims, show the app |
| Emotions | 15 | Write from inside the frustration, not outside it |

The visual hook (35 pts) is where every Mealzy video has under-scored so far. On-screen text and a disruptive opening fix it. That is the most important thing on this brief.

---

## The one-sentence summary

Bold text on screen before you speak + something unexpected happens in the first half second + your first sentence has a real number in it. Everything else follows naturally.
